Summary of the notification
On 25 September 2026, Sofina SA (“Sofina”) received a transparency notification dated 24 September
2026 from which it appears that, on 23 September 2026, BlackRock, Inc. crossed upwards the
statutory threshold of 3% provided for in Article 42 of Sofina’s articles of association.
Following this crossing, BlackRock, Inc. holds, through its controlled undertakings, 1,106,229 voting
rights or equivalent financial instruments, representing 3.01% of the total number of voting rights in
Sofina. This total consists of 823,034 voting rights attached to shares (2.24%) and 283,195 voting rights
that may be acquired through equivalent financial instruments (0.77%).
Content of the notification
The notification dated 24 September 2026 contains the following information:
• Reason for the notification: acquisition or disposal of financial instruments that are treated as
voting securities
• Notification by: a parent undertaking or a controlling person
• Persons subject to the notification requirement: BlackRock, Inc. and its controlled
undertakings listed in the tables below.
• Date on which the threshold was crossed: 23 September 2026
• Threshold crossed: Sofina’s statutory threshold of 3%
• Denominator: 36,696,428
