Company profile
Sofina is Belgian investment company, listed on Euronext Brussels. Sofina’s mission is to
partner with leading entrepreneurs and families, backing them with patient capital and
supportive advice to foster sustainable growth of their businesses. Relationships and
alignment are at the heart of what we do. Sofina has investments in Europe, Asia and the
